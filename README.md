# Thinairatech - Animated Cartoon "Landing Soon"

A vibrant, playful, cartoon-animated landing page designed with a clean **White background, Lush Grass Green landscape, and Rich Colorful Lines & Vehicles** using pure **HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript**.

---

## 🎨 Visual Elements & Colorful Animations

### 1. ✈️ 🚗 🏍️ 🚂 🏔️ Travel & Tourism Scene
- **Mountains & Hills**: Layered lavender-blue alpine peaks with crisp white snow caps and rolling grassy foothills.
- **Railway & Express Train**: Steel tracks along the base with a colorful cartoon locomotive (electric blue, sunny gold, red cowcatcher) pulling an orange carriage with animated steam puffs!
- **Roadway with Car & Bike**:
  - A retro **Cherry Red Beetle Car** cruising with a vacation luggage stack on the roof.
  - A stylish **Teal & Lime Motorbike / Scooter** with a rider wearing an aviator helmet and a fluttering coral scarf.
- **Flying Airplane**: Cartoon sky plane looping through the clouds with a spinning amber propeller and trailing suitcase.

### 2. 🛡️ Cyber-Security Guardian (Upgraded with Neon Lines)
- High-contrast armor with **electric cyan (`#06b6d4`) and neon violet (`#8b5cf6`) circuit line traces**.
- Visor with glowing matrix eyes.
- Multicolored pulsing antenna orb and a rotating holographic security ring.
- Sweeping laser security cone scanning the ground for digital bugs.
- Golden heart padlock emblem.

### 3. 🌱 Agri-Tech Sprout (Upgraded with Vibrant Colors)
- Goofy sprout creature with expressive googly eyes and sunny coral/pink blooming flower petals.
- Smart terra-cotta pot equipped with an interactive **digital moisture gauge**.
- **Smart Agri-Drone** hovering above, spinning violet rotors and dispensing sparkling cyan/green nutrient sprinkles.

### 4. 🏷️ Branding & Subtitle
- **Company Name**: `Thinairatech` with clean, modern typography and a colorful compass-leaf crest.
- **Subtitle**: `landing soon ....` with multi-colored staggered bouncing wave dots.
- **Zero Clutter**: No countdown timer, no forms, no cards, and no written topic names.

---

## 🕹️ Interactive Fun
- **Interactive Vehicles**: Click on the Train, Car, Bike, or Airplane to make them do funny hops with celebratory colorful particle sparks!
- **Eye-Tracking Mascot**: The sprout's googly pupils follow your mouse cursor anywhere on the screen.
- **Click "Boing!" Jump**: Click or tap the Sprout or Cyber Guardian to trigger an elastic squash-and-stretch cartoon jump with color-themed particle bursts.
